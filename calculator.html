How the estimates work

The calculator uses widely cited average body-proportion ratios (H = your height), plus your footwear:

  • Seat height ≈ 0.245 × H + footwear
  • Sitting desk height ≈ seat height + 0.145 × H
  • Standing desk height ≈ 0.63 × H + footwear (+ walking pad deck)
  • Seated eye height ≈ seat height + 0.45 × H

Real bodies vary. Long legs or a long torso can shift these numbers by an inch or two. Treat the results as a starting point, then adjust until your feet rest flat, your shoulders are relaxed, and you can see the top of your screen without tilting your head.
