BIFMA G1-2017 & ANSI Human Factors Reference Principles

Our calculator applies anthropometric regressions derived from the ANSI/BIFMA G1-2017 Ergonomics Guideline for Furniture:

1. Popliteal Seat Pan Formula Seat_Height = (0.245 × Height) + Heel_Allowance
Ensures comfortable seating without excessive pressure on the posterior thigh tissue.
2. Seated Elbow Desk Height Formula Sit_Desk = Seat_Height + (0.145 × Height)
Maintains neutral forearm plane (90° to 105°) without trapezius elevation.
3. Standing Elbow Height Formula Stand_Desk = (0.630 × Height) + Heel_Allowance + Deck_Offset
Aligns keyboard surface directly under wrists during upright or active walking mode.
4. Visual Gaze & Monitor Ray Formula Seated_Eye_Level = Seat_Height + (0.330 × Height)
Positions the top bezel within 0° to -15° downward gaze arc to minimize neck strain.
