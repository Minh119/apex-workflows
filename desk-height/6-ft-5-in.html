Desk & Chair Height for 6'5" (196 cm)

By The Apex Workflows editorial team · Last reviewed Oct 31, 2026

At 6'5", a standard 29–30 inch desk is about 2 inches lower than your ideal sitting height, so it's easy to end up hunched forward, looking down at the screen. Here's what to check.

Your numbers at 6'5"

MeasurementHeight from floorWhat it means
Chair seat height19.8″ (50 cm)Thighs level, feet flat
Sitting desk height31.0″ (79 cm)Keyboard at relaxed elbow height
Standing desk height49.5″ (126 cm)Keyboard at standing elbow height
Standing desk + walking pad51.7″ (131 cm)Adds about 2.25 in for the deck
Top of screen, sitting54.5″ (138 cm)At or slightly below eye level
Top of screen, standing73.1″ (186 cm)At or slightly below eye level

Assumes regular shoes (about 1 in). Barefoot, subtract about 1 in from every number. Estimates from average body proportions: long legs or a long torso can shift them by an inch or two. Fine-tune in our calculator.

What this means for your setup

Buying a standing desk

Check the desk's highest height. You need about 49.5 in for standing (about 51.7 in with a walking pad), and budget frames often top out below that. See our standing desk guide.

Choosing a chair

Look for a seat that rises to about 19.8 in, plus deeper seat-depth adjustment to support your thighs. See our chair guide.

Raising your monitor

Your seated eye height is about 54.5 in from the floor, so most monitor stands are too short. A monitor arm lifts the screen to eye level.

FAQ

What height desk do I need if I'm 6'5"?

About 31.0 in (79 cm) for sitting and 49.5 in (126 cm) for standing, in regular shoes.

What chair seat height is right for 6'5"?

About 19.8 in (50 cm) from the floor to the top of the seat, with your thighs level and feet flat.

How high should my monitor be at 6'5"?

Put the top of the screen at or slightly below 54.5 in (138 cm) from the floor when sitting, about an arm's length away.
