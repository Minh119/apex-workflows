Desk & Chair Height for 5'9" (175 cm)

By The Apex Workflows editorial team · Last reviewed Oct 31, 2026

At 5'9", a standard 29–30 inch desk is close to your ideal sitting height (about 27.9 in), but an inch either way still matters over a full workday. Here's how to fine-tune it.

Your numbers at 5'9"

MeasurementHeight from floorWhat it means
Chair seat height17.9″ (45 cm)Thighs level, feet flat
Sitting desk height27.9″ (71 cm)Keyboard at relaxed elbow height
Standing desk height44.5″ (113 cm)Keyboard at standing elbow height
Standing desk + walking pad46.7″ (119 cm)Adds about 2.25 in for the deck
Top of screen, sitting48.9″ (124 cm)At or slightly below eye level
Top of screen, standing65.6″ (167 cm)At or slightly below eye level

Assumes regular shoes (about 1 in). Barefoot, subtract about 1 in from every number. Estimates from average body proportions: long legs or a long torso can shift them by an inch or two. Fine-tune in our calculator.

What this means for your setup

Fine-tuning a standard desk

Your desk is slightly high for you: raise your chair a little and use a low footrest if your feet stop resting flat. See our footrest and keyboard tray guide.

Buying a standing desk

Most frames cover your range, roughly 27.9 in sitting to 44.5 in standing. If you plan to add a walking pad, make sure the desk reaches about 46.7 in. See our standing desk guide.

Raising your monitor

Your seated eye height is about 48.9 in from the floor. If the top of your screen sits lower than that, a monitor arm or a stand will bring it up.

FAQ

What height desk do I need if I'm 5'9"?

About 27.9 in (71 cm) for sitting and 44.5 in (113 cm) for standing, in regular shoes.

What chair seat height is right for 5'9"?

About 17.9 in (45 cm) from the floor to the top of the seat, with your thighs level and feet flat.

How high should my monitor be at 5'9"?

Put the top of the screen at or slightly below 48.9 in (124 cm) from the floor when sitting, about an arm's length away.
