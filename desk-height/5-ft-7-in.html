Desk & Chair Height for 5'7" (170 cm)

By Minh Tran, mechanical designer · Last reviewed Oct 31, 2026

At 5'7", a standard 29–30 inch desk is about 2 inches higher than your ideal sitting height. That's why so many people end up with raised shoulders or dangling feet. Here's how to fix it.

Your numbers at 5'7"

MeasurementHeight from floorWhat it means
Chair seat height17.4″ (44 cm)Thighs level, feet flat
Sitting desk height27.1″ (69 cm)Keyboard at relaxed elbow height
Standing desk height43.2″ (110 cm)Keyboard at standing elbow height
Standing desk + walking pad45.4″ (115 cm)Adds about 2.25 in for the deck

Top of screen, standing63.7″ (162 cm)At or slightly below eye level

Assumes regular shoes (about 1 in). Barefoot, subtract about 1 in from every number. Estimates from average body proportions: long legs or a long torso can shift them by an inch or two. Fine-tune in our calculator.

What this means for your setup

Fixing a standard desk

Raise your chair until your elbows line up with the desk, then put a footrest under your feet to make up the 2-inch gap. Or lower your keyboard with an under-desk tray. Both options are in our footrest and keyboard tray guide.

Buying a standing desk

Check the desk's lowest height, not just the highest. To sit properly you need it to go down to about 27.1 in, and some frames bottom out higher than that. See our standing desk guide.

Choosing a chair

Look for a seat that goes down to about 17.4 in and a seat depth that's adjustable or on the shallow side, so the edge doesn't press behind your knees. See our chair guide.

FAQ

What height desk do I need if I'm 5'7"?

About 27.1 in (69 cm) for sitting and 43.2 in (110 cm) for standing, in regular shoes.

What chair seat height is right for 5'7"?

About 17.4 in (44 cm) from the floor to the top of the seat, with your thighs level and feet flat.

How high should my monitor be at 5'7"?

Put the top of the screen at or slightly below 47.5 in (121 cm) from the floor when sitting, about an arm's length away.
