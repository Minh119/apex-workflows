Desk & Chair Height for 5'3" (160 cm)

By The Apex Workflows editorial team · Last reviewed Oct 31, 2026

At 5'3", a standard 29–30 inch desk is about 4 inches higher than your ideal sitting height. That's why so many people end up with raised shoulders or dangling feet. Here's how to fix it.

Your numbers at 5'3"

MeasurementHeight from floorWhat it means
Chair seat height16.4″ (42 cm)Thighs level, feet flat
Sitting desk height25.6″ (65 cm)Keyboard at relaxed elbow height
Standing desk height40.7″ (103 cm)Keyboard at standing elbow height
Standing desk + walking pad42.9″ (109 cm)Adds about 2.25 in for the deck
Top of screen, sitting44.8″ (114 cm)At or slightly below eye level
Top of screen, standing60.0″ (152 cm)At or slightly below eye level

Assumes regular shoes (about 1 in). Barefoot, subtract about 1 in from every number. Estimates from average body proportions: long legs or a long torso can shift them by an inch or two. Fine-tune in our calculator.

What this means for your setup

Fixing a standard desk

Raise your chair until your elbows line up with the desk, then put a footrest under your feet to make up the 4-inch gap. Or lower your keyboard with an under-desk tray. Both options are in our footrest and keyboard tray guide.

Buying a standing desk

Check the desk's lowest height, not just the highest. To sit properly you need it to go down to about 25.6 in, and some frames bottom out higher than that. See our standing desk guide.

Choosing a chair

Look for a seat that goes down to about 16.4 in and a seat depth that's adjustable or on the shallow side, so the edge doesn't press behind your knees. See our chair guide.

FAQ

What height desk do I need if I'm 5'3"?

About 25.6 in (65 cm) for sitting and 40.7 in (103 cm) for standing, in regular shoes.

What chair seat height is right for 5'3"?

About 16.4 in (42 cm) from the floor to the top of the seat, with your thighs level and feet flat.

How high should my monitor be at 5'3"?

Put the top of the screen at or slightly below 44.8 in (114 cm) from the floor when sitting, about an arm's length away.
