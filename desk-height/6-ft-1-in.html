Desk & Chair Height for 6'1" (185 cm)

By Minh Tran, mechanical designer · Last reviewed Oct 31, 2026

At 6'1", a standard 29–30 inch desk is close to your ideal sitting height (about 29.5 in), but an inch either way still matters over a full workday. Here's how to fine-tune it.

Your numbers at 6'1"

MeasurementHeight from floorWhat it means
Chair seat height18.9″ (48 cm)Thighs level, feet flat
Sitting desk height29.5″ (75 cm)Keyboard at relaxed elbow height
Standing desk height47.0″ (119 cm)Keyboard at standing elbow height
Standing desk + walking pad49.2″ (125 cm)Adds about 2.25 in for the deck

Assumes regular shoes (about 1 in). Barefoot, subtract about 1 in from every number. Estimates from average body proportions: long legs or a long torso can shift them by an inch or two. Fine-tune in our calculator.

What this means for your setup

Fine-tuning a standard desk

Your desk is about right: set your chair so your elbows line up with the desk surface. See our footrest and keyboard tray guide.

Buying a standing desk

Most frames cover your range, roughly 29.5 in sitting to 47.0 in standing. If you plan to add a walking pad, make sure the desk reaches about 49.2 in. See our standing desk guide.

Raising your monitor

Your seated eye height is about 51.7 in from the floor. If the top of your screen sits lower than that, a monitor arm or a stand will bring it up.

FAQ

What height desk do I need if I'm 6'1"?

About 29.5 in (75 cm) for sitting and 47.0 in (119 cm) for standing, in regular shoes.

What chair seat height is right for 6'1"?

About 18.9 in (48 cm) from the floor to the top of the seat, with your thighs level and feet flat.

How high should my monitor be at 6'1"?

Put the top of the screen at or slightly below 51.7 in (131 cm) from the floor when sitting, about an arm's length away.
