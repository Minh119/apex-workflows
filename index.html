Transparent, Repeatable Evaluation Protocols

We reject vague "out-of-the-box" impressions and uniform placeholder scores. Every product category is evaluated against four weighted engineering criteria documented in our public methodology.

PROTOCOL 01

Spinal & Wrist Biomechanics

Seating and input devices are evaluated for pelvic pressure distribution, L4–L5 lumbar tracking, 57° forearm supination angles, and multi-shift comfort across diverse user heights (5'4" to 6'2").

PROTOCOL 02

Thermal & Material Retention

We record surface temperature rise (°F/°C) across elastomeric meshes, molded foams, and closed-back earcups over continuous 4-hour work blocks in a 72.0°F (22.2°C) controlled room.

PROTOCOL 03

220-Lb Load Deflection @ 46"

Electric standing desks are loaded with 220 lbs of distributed hardware and raised to 46.0 inches standing height to record peak-to-peak lateral deflection (mm) under a 15-Newton impulse.

PROTOCOL 04

Acoustic & Optical Calibration

Microphones, walking pads, and ANC headsets are measured against a 32.0 dBA ambient baseline, while monitors and 4K webcams undergo colorimeter (Delta-E) and low-light CMOS checks.
