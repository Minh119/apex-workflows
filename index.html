Transparent, Repeatable Evaluation Protocols

We reject vague "out-of-the-box" impressions and uniform placeholder scores. Every product category is evaluated against four weighted engineering criteria documented in our public methodology.

PROTOCOL 01

Spinal & Wrist Biomechanics

Seating and input devices are evaluated for pelvic pressure distribution, L4–L5 lumbar tracking, forearm pronation angles, and multi-shift comfort across diverse user anthropometrics.

PROTOCOL 02

Thermal & Material Retention

We record surface temperature rise (°F/°C) across elastomeric meshes, molded foams, and closed-back earcups over continuous 2-to-4-hour work sessions in a 71.6°F (22°C) room.

PROTOCOL 03

Structural Deflection & Load

Standing desks and monitor arms are loaded up to 185–220 lbs at 44-inch standing extension to measure lateral X-axis and front-to-back Y-axis wobble in millimeters (mm).

PROTOCOL 04

Acoustic & Optical Calibration

Microphones, walking pads, and ANC headsets are measured against a 32 dBA ambient baseline, while monitors and 4K webcams undergo colorimeter (Delta-E) and low-light SNR checks.
