How to choose

Resolution: aim for 5120×1440

Lower-resolution 49-inch panels (3840×1080) show noticeably softer text. All picks here are 5120×1440.

OLED vs. IPS for text

OLED offers perfect blacks and fast motion but can show colour fringing on small text. IPS is the safer choice for all-day text work.

USB-C docking

One cable for video, data and laptop charging keeps a desk tidy. Check the wattage is enough for your laptop.

Desk depth and arms

These monitors are wide and heavy. Make sure your desk is deep enough and any monitor arm is rated for the weight.
