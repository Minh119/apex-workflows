Herman Miller Aeron vs. Steelcase Leap V2 (2026): Fit, Specs & Where to Buy

By Minh Tran, mechanical designer · Last reviewed Nov 2, 2026

How this guide was made: this shortlist is based on manufacturer specifications, warranty terms and patterns in published reviews and owner feedback. How we research. Prices change often, so check Amazon for current pricing.

The Aeron and the Leap V2 are the two chairs most often recommended for long workdays, and both are excellent. They suit different bodies and preferences: the Aeron is a breathable mesh chair sold in three sizes, the Leap is a cushioned chair with adjustable seat depth that fits a wider range of people in one size. One thing most comparisons skip: on Amazon US the Leap is sold new from Steelcase's own store, but the Aeron you'll find there is the older Classic design, so it's worth knowing what you're getting.

How to choose

1. Sizing: Aeron comes in A, B and C

The Aeron is sold in three sizes and its seat depth is fixed per size, so choosing the right size matters. Size B fits most adults. The Leap V2 comes in one size with a sliding seat that adjusts depth by about 3 in (15.75–18.75 in).

2. Seat height and fit

Both chairs have a seat height range of roughly 16–20.5 in, which suits people from about 5'1" up without a footrest; see the table below. That's lower than many cheaper ergonomic chairs.

3. Material: mesh vs. foam

The Aeron uses a suspension mesh seat and back that stay cool in warm rooms. The Leap V2 uses cushioned foam that many people find more comfortable for long sessions, but it holds more heat.

4. Back support style

The Aeron's PostureFit SL supports the pelvis and lower back. The Leap's LiveBack flexes to follow the spine and has adjustable lower-back firmness.

5. Load rating and warranty

New, the Leap V2 is rated for 400 lb and the Aeron Size B for 350 lb, and both carry a 12-year manufacturer warranty. Remanufactured and classic chairs carry the seller's warranty instead, so read it before buying.

The shortlist

Most adjustable, cushioned comfort

1. Steelcase Leap V2

The Leap V2 fits a wider range of bodies thanks to its sliding seat (about 3 in of depth adjustment) and adjustable lower-back firmness, and its cushioned seat is comfortable over long days. On Amazon US it's sold new through the Steelcase store; if you want to spend less, Crandall Office also sells remanufactured Leap V2s there with its own 12-year warranty (see below).

  • Sold new on Amazon, plus a cheaper remanufactured option

Trade-offs

Warm rooms and mesh lovers

2. Herman Miller Aeron (Size B)

The Aeron's mesh suspension keeps you cool, and its design has a long track record. On Amazon US you'll mostly find the Classic Aeron, the version Herman Miller made until 2016, sold new-old-stock or renewed. Compared with today's Remastered Aeron, the Classic has an older mesh, a pad under the front of the seat and a different lumbar option. If you want the current model and Herman Miller's 12-year warranty, buy it from Herman Miller or an authorized dealer.

What we like

  • Breathable mesh seat and back
  • Three sizes for different bodies
  • Classic version often costs less

Trade-offs

  • Seat depth not adjustable
  • Classic/renewed warranty depends on the seller

Skip it if: You're between sizes or want adjustable seat depth.

New, remanufactured or classic: what you're buying

  • New: sold by Herman Miller, Steelcase and their authorized dealers, with the full 12-year manufacturer warranty. On Amazon US, the new Leap is sold through Steelcase's store; the current Remastered Aeron isn't listed there.
  • Remanufactured (Leap V2): the remanufactured Leap V2 from Crandall Office is a used chair rebuilt by Crandall Office with original Steelcase parts, backed by Crandall's 12-year warranty and 30-day returns. A good way to get a Leap for less, with the warranty coming from Crandall rather than Steelcase.
  • Classic or renewed (Aeron on Amazon): the pre-2016 Aeron design. It's still a capable chair, but check exactly who stands behind the warranty and for how long before you buy.

FAQ

Which is better for short people, Aeron or Leap?

Both seats go down to about 16 in, which suits people from about 5'1". The Leap's adjustable seat depth usually makes it the easier fit for shorter legs; with the Aeron, choose Size A or B.

Which is cooler in a warm room?

The Aeron. Its mesh seat and back let air through, while the Leap's foam holds more heat.

Is a remanufactured Leap V2 worth it?

Often, yes. Crandall's listing says it gets a new cylinder, casters, arm pads, fabric and seat foam, and Crandall backs the whole chair with a 12-year warranty, at a lower price than new. Just note the warranty comes from Crandall, not Steelcase.
