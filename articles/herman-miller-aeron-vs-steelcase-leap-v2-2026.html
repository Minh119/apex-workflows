Herman Miller Aeron vs. Steelcase Leap V2 (2026): Which Should You Buy?

The Aeron and the Leap V2 are the two chairs most often recommended for long workdays, and both are excellent. They suit different bodies and preferences, though. This comparison uses manufacturer specifications and the long track record of both chairs to help you pick.

How to choose

Sizing: Aeron comes in A, B and C

The Aeron is sold in three sizes and its seat depth is fixed per size, so choosing the right size matters. Size B fits most adults. The Leap V2 comes in one size with adjustable seat depth.

Material: mesh vs. foam

The Aeron uses a suspension mesh seat and back that stay cool in warm rooms. The Leap V2 uses cushioned foam that many people find more comfortable for long sessions, but it holds more heat.

Back support style

The Aeron's PostureFit SL supports the pelvis and lower back. The Leap's LiveBack flexes to mimic the spine and has adjustable lower-back firmness.

Warranty

Both come with long manufacturer warranties. Check the current terms and the seller, because some marketplace listings are not authorized dealers and may not carry the full warranty.

The shortlist

Most adjustable, cushioned comfort

1. Steelcase Leap V2

The Leap V2 fits a wider range of bodies thanks to adjustable seat depth, and its cushioned seat is comfortable over long days.

What we like

  • Adjustable seat depth
  • Adjustable lower-back firmness
  • Cushioned seat

Trade-offs

  • Foam runs warmer than mesh
  • Plain styling

Skip it if: Your office is hot or you dislike foam seats.

Warm rooms and mesh lovers

2. Herman Miller Aeron (Size B)

The Aeron's mesh suspension keeps you cool, and its design has a long track record. It's the better pick for warm climates if you choose the right size.

What we like

  • Breathable mesh seat and back
  • PostureFit SL pelvic support
  • Three sizes for different bodies

Trade-offs

  • Seat depth is not adjustable
  • Firm seat edge some users dislike

Skip it if: You're between sizes or want adjustable seat depth.
