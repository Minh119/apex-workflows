How to choose

Light your face first

A simple light in front of you improves any webcam more than an upgrade does.

Sensor size for dim rooms

Larger sensors produce cleaner images in low light.

Tracking vs. fixed

Gimbal webcams follow you around the room; fixed webcams are simpler and have no moving parts.

Eye level

Mount the camera at eye level so you're not looking down at the lens. Our calculator estimates your seated eye height.
