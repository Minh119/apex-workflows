In an untreated home office, a dynamic microphone usually sounds better than a condenser because it picks up less room echo and background noise. All four picks here offer both USB and XLR, so you can start simple and upgrade later.

How to choose

Dynamic for untreated rooms

Dynamic mics reject more room sound. Position them close to your mouth, about 2–4 inches.

USB + XLR keeps options open

USB is plug-and-play; XLR lets you move to an audio interface later without replacing the mic.

Get a boom arm

A boom arm keeps the mic close to your mouth without it blocking your screen or picking up desk bumps.

The shortlist

Shure's hybrid USB-C/XLR dynamic mic, with onboard processing and a touch panel. It's built for podcasters who want broadcast-style sound with minimal setup.
