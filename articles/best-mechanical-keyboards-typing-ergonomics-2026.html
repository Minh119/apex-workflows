For long typing days, layout and posture matter as much as switches. A compact board keeps your mouse closer, a low profile reduces wrist extension, and a split board lets your shoulders relax. This shortlist covers each approach.

How to choose

Smaller layouts keep your mouse closer

A 75% or tenkeyless board shortens the reach to your mouse, which can ease shoulder strain.

Low profile or a wrist rest

Tall mechanical boards can push your wrists upward. Either choose a low-profile board or use a palm rest.

Split boards for shoulder width

Split keyboards let each hand sit at shoulder width. They take a week or two to adapt to.

Programmability

QMK/VIA support lets you remap keys, which is useful for coders who want layers and macros.
