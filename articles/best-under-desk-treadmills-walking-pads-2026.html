How to choose

Raise your desk for the deck

A walking pad adds a few inches of height. Our calculator has a walking-pad option to adjust your desk height.

Walking speed is slow

Most people type comfortably around 1–2 mph. You don't need running speeds for desk use.

Noise and floors

Use a mat to cut vibration, especially in apartments or on calls.

Weight limits and belt width

Check the user weight limit and belt width against your stride before buying.
