How to choose

Check the height range against your body

Your standing desk height should land roughly at your standing elbow height. Use our desk height calculator and confirm the desk's maximum height clears that number, with room for shoes or a walking pad.

Prefer dual motors for heavy setups

Dual-motor frames lift more evenly and usually carry higher weight ratings. If you run two monitors on arms plus a laptop dock, a dual-motor frame is the safer choice.

Three-stage legs reduce wobble at height

Wobble is worst at maximum extension. Three-stage legs and wider feet generally feel steadier than two-stage designs when you type standing up.

Read the warranty, not just the spec sheet

Frame and motor warranties vary a lot between brands. A long motor warranty is the best signal that a manufacturer expects the desk to last.

FAQ

What height should my standing desk be?

Roughly your standing elbow height, so your forearms are level when you type. Our calculator estimates it from your height, including shoes and an optional walking pad.

Single or dual motor?

A single motor is fine for a laptop and one light monitor. For two monitors, monitor arms or anything heavy, a dual-motor frame lifts more smoothly and usually carries a higher weight rating.

How long should I stand each day?

There's no magic number. Most people do best switching between sitting and standing through the day rather than standing for hours at a time.
