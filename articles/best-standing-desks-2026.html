The Single vs. Dual Motor Reality Check

Single-motor desks connect both legs via a hex spindle drive. Over time, friction unevenly wears one gear, causing desktop pitch imbalance. Dual-motor architectures employ synchronized hall-effect sensors in both columns, guaranteeing level ascents even with off-center desktop loads.
