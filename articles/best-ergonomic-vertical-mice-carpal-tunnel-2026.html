Best Ergonomic Vertical Mice (2026): Pick by Hand Size

A vertical mouse turns your hand into a handshake position, which reduces forearm twisting. The most important factor is fitting the mouse to your hand size. This guide shortlists five options and explains how to measure your hand.

How to choose

Measure your hand

Measure from the wrist crease to the tip of your middle finger. Under about 7.5 in (19 cm) usually suits smaller mice like the Logitech Lift; longer hands suit the MX Vertical.

Steeper isn't always better

More vertical angles reduce forearm rotation further but take longer to get used to. Start around 57° if you're new to vertical mice.

Left-handed?

Only some models come in a left-handed version. The Logitech Lift and Evoluent offer one.

This isn't medical treatment

An ergonomic mouse may make you more comfortable, but it does not treat carpal tunnel syndrome. See a clinician for persistent pain or numbness.

The shortlist

Small to medium hands

1. Logitech Lift Vertical

The Lift uses a 57° angle in a compact body, has quiet clicks, and comes in a dedicated left-handed version.

What we like

  • Sized for small-to-medium hands
  • Left-handed version available
  • Bluetooth or Logi Bolt receiver

Trade-offs

  • Too small for large hands
  • Uses an AA battery rather than charging

Skip it if: Your hand is longer than about 7.5 in.

2. Logitech MX Master 3S

Not a vertical mouse, but a sculpted shape that supports your palm, with a fast scroll wheel and thumb wheel. A good step if a full vertical feels too unfamiliar.

  • Quiet clicks, USB-C charging

Trade-offs

  • Less forearm rotation reduction than a vertical mouse
  • Right-handed only

Skip it if: You specifically want to reduce forearm twisting.
