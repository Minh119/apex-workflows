Standard fixed desks are about 29–30 inches tall. For many people, especially anyone under about 5'10", that's too high to type with relaxed shoulders. You have two fixes: raise your chair and support your feet with a footrest, or lower your keyboard with an under-desk tray. This guide covers both.

How to choose

First, find out how big the gap is

Use our calculator to get your ideal sitting desk height and compare it with your desk. If your desk is 2–4 inches too high, either fix works. Much more than that and a keyboard tray, or a height-adjustable desk, is usually the better answer.

Footrest: raise the chair, then support your feet

Set your chair so your elbows are level with the desk, then measure the gap between the floor and your feet. That gap is the footrest height you need. Adjustable-height footrests make this easy to dial in.

Keyboard tray: lower the keyboard instead

A tray lowers your keyboard and mouse below the desktop so you can keep your chair at a normal height. It works well when several people share a desk or when your chair can't go high enough.

Check your desk before buying a tray

Clamp-on trays need a desk edge they can grip (check the thickness range on the listing). Track-style drawers screw into the underside, so they need a solid wood or particleboard desk and enough clear depth underneath.

FAQ

Do I need a footrest?

If your feet don't rest flat on the floor once your chair is high enough for your elbows to reach the desk, yes. Dangling feet put pressure on the back of your thighs.

How high should a footrest be?

As high as the gap between the floor and your feet when your thighs are level and your elbows line up with the desk. Adjustable footrests let you match that gap.

Footrest or keyboard tray: which is better?

A footrest is cheaper and needs no installation. A keyboard tray is better when the desk is shared, when your chair can't go high enough, or when you'd rather keep your feet on the floor.

Is a keyboard tray better than a standing desk?

If your only problem is a desk that's a few inches too high, a tray is a cheap fix. If you also want to stand, a height-adjustable desk solves both. See our standing desk guide.
