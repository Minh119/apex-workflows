How to choose

Seat height first

With feet flat, your thighs should be roughly level. Our calculator estimates your ideal seat height from your body height.

Seat depth matters more than people think

You want 2–3 finger-widths between the seat edge and the back of your knees. Adjustable seat depth helps both shorter and taller users.

Adjustable lumbar beats fixed lumbar

Lower backs differ. Height-adjustable or self-adjusting lumbar support fits more people than a fixed bump.

Mesh runs cooler, foam feels softer

Mesh breathes better in warm rooms; cushioned foam spreads pressure more evenly. Pick based on your climate and preference.

FAQ

What seat height do I need?

With your feet flat, your thighs should be roughly level. Our calculator gives a starting point based on your height.

My desk is too high for my chair. What should I do?

Raise the chair until your elbows line up with the desk, then support your feet with a footrest, or lower your keyboard with a tray. See our footrest and keyboard tray guide.

Is mesh or foam better?

Mesh stays cooler in warm rooms; foam spreads pressure more evenly and feels softer. Neither is better for everyone.
