How to choose

Comfort over specs

Weight and clamp force decide whether you'll wear them all day. Heavier headphones like the AirPods Max can tire your neck.

Battery life

If you travel or forget to charge, long battery life (like the Momentum 4's) matters more than small ANC differences.

Ecosystem

AirPods Max integrate best with Apple devices; Sony, Bose and Sennheiser work well across platforms with multipoint pairing.
