About The Apex Workflows

The Apex Workflows is a small, independent website that publishes buying guides for home office and ergonomic equipment.

What we do

We research standing desks, office chairs, monitors, input devices and audio/video gear for people who work from home. Each guide tries to answer a practical question: which option fits your body, your space and your budget, and what are the trade-offs?

What we don't do

We don't currently run a hands-on testing lab, and we don't publish measurements or scores we haven't taken ourselves. Our guides are built from manufacturer specifications, warranty terms and published reviews and owner feedback. We say so at the top of every guide. If we test products ourselves in the future, we'll label that clearly and explain how.

How we make money

Some links on this site are Amazon affiliate links. If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Manufacturers can't pay to be included or ranked.

Corrections

Spotted a mistake, an outdated spec or a broken link? Email editorial@theapexworkflows.com and we'll fix it.
