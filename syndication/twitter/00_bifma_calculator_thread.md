1/5 🪑 95% of remote workers use a desk that is ergonomically too high.

Standard desks are 29"-30" tall—designed for a 6'3" male. If you are 5'4"-5'10", this forces trapezius shrug and wrist extension.

---

2/5 📐 The Ergonomic Golden Ratio:

• Chair Height = Popliteal height + shoe sole (16"-19" for most)
• Seated Desk = Seated elbow height (usually 24.5"-27.5", NOT 30"!)
• Standing Desk = Standing elbow level (38"-44")
• Top of Monitor = Eye level

---

3/5 🚶 Using an under-desk walking pad?

Don't forget the deck offset. A treadmill deck adds +2.0" to +2.5" of elevation under your feet. Your standing desk must raise by that exact amount to keep wrists neutral.
