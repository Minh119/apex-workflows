How We Research Our Guides

Every guide on this site is research-based. Here's exactly what that means.

Sources we use

  • Manufacturer specifications: dimensions, weight ratings, height ranges, connectivity and warranty terms, taken from official product pages and listings.
  • Published reviews and owner feedback: we look for consistent patterns (for example, repeated reports of wobble or durability problems) rather than single opinions.
  • General ergonomics guidance: body-proportion estimates and widely used workstation setup recommendations. Our calculator uses standard anthropometric ratios and gives a starting point, not a prescription.

How we pick and order products

We shortlist products that are widely available in the US, have a clear advantage for a specific kind of buyer, and have no widespread reliability complaints. Each pick gets a "best for" label and a "skip it if" note, because no product suits everyone.

Prices and availability

Prices change frequently, so we don't list them. Use the Amazon links for current pricing and availability.

Health note

Ergonomic equipment can make work more comfortable, but our content is not medical advice. If you have persistent pain, numbness or a diagnosed condition, talk to a qualified clinician.
